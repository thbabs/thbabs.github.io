About me

Hey there, I’m really glad you stopped by.

I started out as an aerospace engineering graduate and worked in the airline industry for about 2 years. From there I moved into data, first data entry and then data analysis, and I went back to school for a master’s degree in artificial intelligence.

I’m now Lead AI Developer at Solverdeck, with 5+ years of building machine learning models, AI systems and frameworks, from prototype to production.

Alongside this, I contribute to open-source projects.

Some things I’ve built recently:

Multivrs, the all-in-one platform to build, launch, and run your business.

KontinueAI, one workspace for every AI model, with your chats carried over.

I also work with SMEs to automate their repetitive tasks, and I share tips on using AI tools to save time and cost.

I believe AI should feel simple. The best tools quietly take the busywork off your plate, so you can spend your time on the work that actually matters.

You’ll probably find me listening to music, enjoying the outdoors, trying new food, or checking out art.

If you’re building something, or just want to chat about getting more done with AI, I’d love to hear from you.

Thanks for stopping by, it means a lot.

Cheers,
